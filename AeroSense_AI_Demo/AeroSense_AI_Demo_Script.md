# AeroSense AI — Product Demonstration Video Script & Scene Breakdown
**Project:** AeroSense AI — Global Urban Air Quality Intelligence Platform  
**Challenge:** H2S Hack2Skill Challenge 6 – Urban Air Quality Assistant  
**Target Video Duration:** ~2 minutes 45 seconds (165 seconds)  
**Resolution:** 1920 × 1080 (16:9 Full HD, 30 FPS)  
**Audio:** Professional English Narration (Neural Voice), AAC Audio  

---

## Scene 1: Opening & Problem Statement
* **Timecode:** 0:00 – 0:15 (Duration: 15 seconds)
* **Visual Focus:** High-resolution AeroSense AI title branding card with animated subtitle *"Understand the air. Predict the risk. Make better decisions."*, transitioning into the live application workspace.
* **Narration:**
  > "Air quality affects how we live, travel, and spend time outdoors. But environmental readings alone do not always tell us what they mean or how we should respond. AeroSense AI brings air-quality intelligence, environmental data, and AI-powered interpretation together in one unified platform."

---

## Scene 2: Overview Dashboard
* **Timecode:** 0:15 – 0:40 (Duration: 25 seconds)
* **Visual Focus:** Live Overview Command Center for Tokyo and London terminals. Displays verified physical ground station telemetry, diagnostic AQI dial across US EPA, India CPCB, and European EAQI, the WHO 2021 concentration matrix with exposure ratio bars, and the 24-hour predictive timeline.
* **Narration:**
  > "AeroSense AI provides a centralized view of available air-quality information. Users can understand pollution levels, inspect pollutant readings, and examine the monitoring data available for their selected location. The platform makes data sources, timestamps, and WHO guideline ratios visible, helping users understand the freshness and reliability of the information."

---

## Scene 3: Global Air-Quality Map
* **Timecode:** 0:40 – 1:05 (Duration: 25 seconds)
* **Visual Focus:** Full-bleed MapLibre GL GIS spatial canvas with watermark-free Esri Canvas tiles. Dynamic panning across physical ground stations with authentic AQI pins, station inspector popups, atmospheric variable switches (PM2.5, PM10, NO2), and the Atmosphere 360 temporal animation scrubber.
* **Narration:**
  > "With the interactive global map, users can explore available monitoring stations geographically. Selecting a physical ground station reveals verified environmental readings, coordinates, and observation freshness, making it easy to understand how air quality varies across monitored urban corridors."

---

## Scene 4: Pollution Analytics & City Comparison
* **Timecode:** 1:05 – 1:30 (Duration: 25 seconds)
* **Visual Focus:** Transitioning into the Air Intelligence Observatory analytics workspace. Demonstrates continuous 72-hour historical & forecast spline charts, statistical atmospheric summaries, deterministic anomaly surge detection (>60% above baseline), and one-click CSV export. Then switches to City Comparison for side-by-side atmospheric benchmarking across Tokyo, London, and Delhi.
* **Narration:**
  > "Beyond current readings, AeroSense AI helps users explore environmental trends. Its analytics observatory provides continuous historical and forecast trajectories with deterministic surge detection, while city comparison tools enable side-by-side benchmarking across global metropolitan areas."

---

## Scene 5: Grounded GenAI Assistant (Google Gemini 2.5 Flash)
* **Timecode:** 1:30 – 2:00 (Duration: 30 seconds)
* **Visual Focus:** Live interaction with the AI Assistant. Typing: *"Explain the current air quality in Tokyo and what precautions I should take for outdoor running."* Live processing and generation of a grounded environmental briefing citing verified PM2.5 levels, AQI classification, activity safety ratings, and medical disclaimers.
* **Narration:**
  > "The integrated Gemini-powered assistant adds contextual environmental intelligence. Instead of simply displaying raw numbers, it interprets atmospheric conditions and delivers actionable activity guidance. Every statement is strictly grounded in real telemetry retrieved through backend function calling, with zero hallucinated figures."

---

## Scene 6: Health Guidance, Alerts, Reports & Theme Engine
* **Timecode:** 2:00 – 2:25 (Duration: 25 seconds)
* **Visual Focus:** Showcases the Health & Activity Advisor across outdoor running, cycling, walking, and sensitive demographic guidance. Demonstrates Intelligent Alerts threshold configuration and breach event feeds. Displays Environmental Compliance Reports with ReportLab vector PDF export. Toggles seamlessly between scientific Light and Dark themes.

  > "Additional capabilities turn environmental data into practical insights. Users can consult evidence-based activity guidance, configure threshold-based pollution alerts, access compliance reports, and seamlessly toggle between scientific light and dark workstation themes."
